Regional School Bus Co beginner guide

Start at the Bus Yard

Head to the Bus Yard before trying to begin a normal drive. An older Spawn Anywhere pass describes bypassing the yard, but that pass is currently off sale. New players should not expect to summon a bus from any point on the map.

The precise spawn buttons and yard layout are not published here because they can change. Follow the current labels and prompts in your server.

Field note

If you cannot find a bus, reset your plan around the Bus Yard instead of searching for an unverified spawn shortcut.

Choose a public bus and set a number

Standard public buses can be driven by anyone in the public game, so a new player does not need a CDL to begin. Choose an available public bus and set a bus number through the current interface.

Assigned and premium vehicles follow different community rules. Do not assume every visible bus is public, free, or available to your account.

Drive and transport students

The verified premise is to drive a school bus and transport students. Follow the live route and student prompts shown in your server.

Exact route names, stop order, rewards, and platform-specific controls are intentionally omitted because the available sources do not verify stable current values.

Keep enough fuel for the drive

Refueling is a confirmed part of play. Watch the live fuel display and refuel when the game indicates that it is needed.

Fuel-station locations, refueling controls, fuel prices, and the exact result of running out are not verified here. Use the current map and interface rather than an old fixed instruction.

First-session checklist

  • Begin at the Bus Yard.
  • Choose a standard public bus and set a bus number in the live interface.
  • Confirm the controls for your device before joining traffic.
  • Transport students using the current route prompts.
  • Watch the fuel display and refuel when needed.
  • Finish the route, then repeat or join an organized route session.

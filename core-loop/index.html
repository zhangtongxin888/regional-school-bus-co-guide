Regional School Bus Co core gameplay loop

Follow the Bus Yard-to-finish cycle: choose a public bus and number, transport students, refuel, finish, and repeat.

Bus Yard → bus and number → students → fuel → finish

Start at the Bus Yard, choose a standard public bus, set a bus number, run a student-transport route through Upstate Region, refuel as needed, finish, and repeat. An organized route session can be an alternative next step when one is available.

Use live prompts for precise timing. Stable route names, stop order, rewards, and exact completion messages were not verified and are not invented here.

Let the live route prompt confirm success

Finish the route according to the current on-screen direction before beginning another cycle. This guide does not publish a fixed reward amount or a guessed completion screen.

Use fuel as a continuity check

Refueling is a confirmed part of the experience. Treat fuel as something to check during the driving loop, while relying on the current interface for the precise threshold and refueling process.

Warning states, station locations, fuel prices, and the recovery flow are not verified here and may change.

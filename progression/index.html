Early game: build a reliable drive

Begin with the confirmed fundamentals: choose a bus number, learn the controls for your device, transport students, and refuel when needed. Do not rush toward advanced systems before you can complete the basic driving flow reliably.

Regional School Bus Co supports Mobile, PC, Xbox, and PS5. Exact controls are intentionally omitted until each current platform layout can be verified.

Mid game: routes and vehicle assignments

Route permissions and vehicle assignments are useful directions for later research, but their current rules are not yet confirmed.

Eligibility, progression order, costs, and current restrictions are not verified, so this guide does not recommend a specific purchase or assignment path.

Advanced: CDL and BD Edition

CDL and BD Edition can be treated as advanced directions, not as guaranteed immediate unlocks. Exact qualifications, prices, benefits, and access rules remain unverified.

The community group points drivers to the Training Facility for the CDL path, which leads toward route permissions, BD Edition, fleet assignment, and rank progression. Current tests, costs, and eligibility are not published here.

Use a high-performance device when possible

The official game information recommends a high-performance device. If the experience is unstable, lower in-game graphics settings and close heavy background apps before assuming a gameplay mechanic is broken.

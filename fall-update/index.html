Regional School Bus Co Fall Update 2026

Part 1 of the fall update is live. Here’s what it added, where to find it, and what we know about the Corn Maze 2026 badge.

4-minute readUpdated October 1, 2026

Quick answer

The fall update (part 1) added a pumpkin farm with pumpkin-tossing and pumpkin-weighing areas, the Upstate Drive-In Theater, new buildings and plazas, map updates, fall lighting and foliage, plus bug fixes. That list comes from the in-game update log as read in the ShayArson video “ROBLOX | Regional School Bus Co | FALL IS HERE!” (late September 2026). The same log says a part 2 with Halloween content is still to come.

Roblox lists the game’s latest update as 2026-09-30 20:39 UTC.

Corn Maze 2026 badge

The official badge Corn Maze 2026 reads: “This badge was unlocked by completing the corn maze during October 2026.” Roblox’s badge API showed it awarded to 0 players on October 1, 2026, so nobody has been able to finish the maze yet.

BadgeOfficial conditionStatus 2026-10-01

In the late-September video the maze entrance was closed, and the creator guessed it opens with part 2. Treat the opening date as unconfirmed until the maze is open in your server.

Where to find the fall locations

  1. Spawn a bus at the Bus Yard (or walk).
  2. Open the minimap. The pumpkin farm has its own marker, so use it to turn around if you take the wrong road.
  3. The Upstate Drive-In Theater showed movie titles on its sign. In the video it came up shortly after the river area, so use the minimap if you can’t see it.
  4. The pumpkin-weighing contest is a display of results (in the video it had already happened), not a minigame you play.
Field note

An NPC at the farm asks a riddle: “I am full of rows but have no seeds…” The answer that worked in the video was cornfield.

Free cash: the group reward

In the same video, an in-game group-membership check for the Regional Bus Community Roblox group paid $175 in-game cash. That’s a single observation, so the amount may change. There are no redeem codes; see the codes answer.

Fall update lag

The fall video showed lag spikes of 15–20 seconds, assets loading late and floating terrain near the farm. The game page warns it’s not built for low-end devices. If you lag, turn off Clouds and realistic light glow, lower graphics, and rejoin a less busy server (max 8 players).

Earlier in 2026: the summer update

The summer 2026 update (reviewed in “ROBLOX | Regional School Bus Co | 2026 Summer Update Review”) added route GPS, a minimap, song radio, terrain grass, stop-sign light reflections, a new Vision drive sound, a construction site and a carnival field-trip spot with rides. The 2026 Next Gen in that log was a roster bus, and the log teased “free third gens for everyone” for a later update. Whether that arrived isn’t confirmed.

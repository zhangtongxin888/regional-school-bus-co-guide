What should I do first in Regional School Bus Co?+

Start at the Bus Yard, choose a standard public bus and set a number, confirm the controls for your device, transport students, refuel as needed, and finish the route before repeating.

What is the core gameplay loop?+

Bus Yard → choose a public bus and number → transport students → refuel as needed → finish → repeat. Exact route names, stop order, and rewards are not published because stable values were not verified.

Do beginners need a CDL?+

No. Standard public buses can be driven by anyone. The CDL is a community progression path through the Training Facility that leads toward route permissions, BD Edition, fleet assignment, and rank progression.

Can I spawn a bus anywhere?+

Plan to start at the Bus Yard. The older Spawn Anywhere pass is currently off sale, so this guide does not promise map-wide spawning.

Which platforms are supported?+

Regional School Bus Co supports Mobile, PC, Xbox, and PS5. Exact controls are platform-specific and still need verification.

Why does the game recommend a high-performance device?+

The official game information recommends a high-performance device. If performance is poor, try lower graphics settings and close heavy background apps; exact device requirements are not published here.

How do CDL, route permissions, vehicle assignments, and BD Edition work?+

The Training Facility CDL path leads toward route permissions, BD Edition, fleet assignment, and rank progression. Bus Driver+ members may qualify for an assigned bus, but current prices, qualifications, number ranges, and exact access rules can change and are not fixed here.
